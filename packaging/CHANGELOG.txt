FENIX COLOUR SUITE  -  CHANGELOG

1.0.1
- Update notice: the footer shows "UPDATE x.y.z AVAILABLE" when a newer version is out. Click it for
  the download page, or to switch the check off. It asks GitHub at most once a day and sends nothing
  about you or your sessions.
- Lighter on the CPU: the display no longer redraws its background on every frame, which halves the
  editor's idle CPU use.
- macOS builds come from a newer build machine (macOS 15).

1.0.0
- First release: Fenix Bus, Drive, Shelf EQ, HF Comp, Width and Iron.
- Windows (VST3), macOS (VST3 and AU, universal) and Linux x86_64 (VST3 and LV2).
- Factory presets for every plugin, plus user presets.
- Click-free bypass in every plugin and per-stage switches in Fenix Bus.
- Every build passes pluginval at its strictest level; the AUs also pass Apple's auval.
